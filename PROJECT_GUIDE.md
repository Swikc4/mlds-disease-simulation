## How we will divide the work

Five teammates and I will choose roles at the meeting. For the first combined assignment, we need explanations, SIR coding, SEIR coding, charts, and testing. I will teach the overview, coordinate outputs, review pull requests, interpret results and integrate approved work. We will rotate tasks in later weeks.

## Working together on GitHub

We will post only the current assignment under `assignments/`. Everyone works on their own branch and submits a pull request. After review, I will combine accepted sections into one final notebook under `our_work/`. GitHub does not automatically combine separate notebooks. We will keep checked findings under `results/` and sources under `research/`.

## Research and data

SIR and SEIR begin with teaching assumptions, not real patients. The town will generate simulated data. We already have the starting CDC and published sources from our proposal in [research/README.md](research/README.md). Every disease number must be marked SOURCED, DERIVED or ASSUMPTION, with an explanation.

## Required core and optional work

The core is a working interactive simulation for three diseases, reviewed charts, and simple machine learning tested on unseen simulated runs. Historical NYC COVID 19 comparison and gradient boosting are optional. We will not sacrifice a working demo to add them.
